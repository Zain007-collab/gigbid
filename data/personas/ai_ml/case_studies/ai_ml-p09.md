# Document Q&A assistant for a e-commerce team

**Client:** a e-commerce organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2022.

## Problem
The client needed help with document q&a assistant for a e-commerce team. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using RAG, LangChain, FastAPI. Supporting pieces included managed vector database; evaluation harness for the bot.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We cut average lookup time from 9 minutes to 1 seconds.
