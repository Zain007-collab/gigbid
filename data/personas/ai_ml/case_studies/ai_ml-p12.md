# Visual defect detection for a marketing agency line

**Client:** a marketing agency organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2021.

## Problem
The client needed help with visual defect detection for a marketing agency line. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using PyTorch. Supporting pieces included containerised deployment; Python REST API.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We reached 37% precision at 49% recall on held-out images.
