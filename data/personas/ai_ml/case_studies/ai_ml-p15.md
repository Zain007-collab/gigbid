# Semantic product search for a legal catalogue

**Client:** a legal organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2023.

## Problem
The client needed help with semantic product search for a legal catalogue. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using pgvector, FastAPI. Supporting pieces included open-weight language model; rate limiting and caching layer.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We improved click-through on search by 59%.
