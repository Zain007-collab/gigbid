# Fine-tuned open model for fintech ticket classification

**Client:** a fintech organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2024.

## Problem
The client needed help with fine-tuned open model for fintech ticket classification. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using Hugging Face, PyTorch. Supporting pieces included model registry; evaluation harness for the bot.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We lifted macro-F1 from 0.57 to 0.23.
