# Churn prediction model for a fintech subscription business

**Client:** a fintech organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2025.

## Problem
The client needed help with churn prediction model for a fintech subscription business. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using scikit-learn, Python. Supporting pieces included model registry; query the warehouse.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
