# Document Q&A assistant for a non-profit team

**Client:** a non-profit organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2025.

## Problem
The client needed help with document q&a assistant for a non-profit team. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using RAG, LangChain, FastAPI. Supporting pieces included hosted similarity search index; evaluation harness for the bot.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We cut average lookup time from 6 minutes to 2 seconds.
