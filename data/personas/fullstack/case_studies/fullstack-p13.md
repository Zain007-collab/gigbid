# Booking backend for a SaaS platform

**Client:** a SaaS organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2023.

## Problem
The client needed help with booking backend for a saas platform. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using FastAPI, PostgreSQL. Supporting pieces included scheduled background jobs; in-memory caching.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We cut double-bookings to zero across 4,000 bookings.
