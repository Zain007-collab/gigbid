# Subscription billing for a SaaS product

**Client:** a SaaS organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2024.

## Problem
The client needed help with subscription billing for a saas product. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using Stripe, Node.js. Supporting pieces included SQL database design; in-memory caching.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We recovered 53% of failed payments with dunning emails.
