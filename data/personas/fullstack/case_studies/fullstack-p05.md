# Booking backend for a manufacturing platform

**Client:** a manufacturing organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2025.

## Problem
The client needed help with booking backend for a manufacturing platform. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using FastAPI, PostgreSQL. Supporting pieces included background task queue; in-memory caching.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We cut double-bookings to zero across 7,000 bookings.
