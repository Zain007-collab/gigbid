# Customer dashboard for a logistics SaaS

**Client:** a logistics organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2024.

## Problem
The client needed help with customer dashboard for a logistics saas. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using Next.js, TypeScript. Supporting pieces included type-safe ORM; relational database; responsive styling system.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We cut page load from 5s to 2s.
