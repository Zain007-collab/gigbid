# Real-time collaboration board for a travel team

**Client:** a travel organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2026.

## Problem
The client needed help with real-time collaboration board for a travel team. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using React, Websockets. Supporting pieces included rate limiting and caching layer; server-side JS.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We supported 700 concurrent editors with sub-second sync.
