# Real-time collaboration board for a logistics team

**Client:** a logistics organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2024.

## Problem
The client needed help with real-time collaboration board for a logistics team. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using React, Websockets. Supporting pieces included rate limiting and caching layer; server-side JS.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We supported 300 concurrent editors with sub-second sync.
