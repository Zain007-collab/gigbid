# Warehouse modelling for a SaaS team

**Client:** a SaaS organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2025.

## Problem
The client needed help with warehouse modelling for a saas team. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did

I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.
