# Marketing attribution report for a healthcare brand

**Client:** a healthcare organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2021.

## Problem
The client needed help with marketing attribution report for a healthcare brand. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using Looker Studio, Bigquery. Supporting pieces included query the warehouse; shared spreadsheet automation.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We showed 62% of spend went to low-return channels.
