# Warehouse modelling for a healthcare team

**Client:** a healthcare organisation (anonymised). **Year:** 2022.

## Problem
The client needed help with warehouse modelling for a healthcare team. Their existing process was manual and slow.

## What I did
I designed and delivered the solution using dbt, Snowflake. Supporting pieces included complex queries on our data; DAG-based scheduling.
I wrote documentation and handed over with a recorded walkthrough.

## Result
We cut dashboard refresh from 4 hours to 1 minutes.
